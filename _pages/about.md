Hi, I am Samarth Raman Ghanate. I recently finished my M.Sc. in 
Electromobility Engineering at Friedrich-Alexander University (FAU) 
Erlangen-Nürnberg. I work as a Research Assistant at Siemens 
Healthineers, where I build multi-sensor perception systems for 
clinical environments.

I like working on things that actually run on hardware. Most of my 
work is about making machines perceive and understand the world around 
them, whether through sensor fusion, semantic segmentation, or 
real-time deployment on edge devices.

My master's thesis looked at sensor selection and positioning for 
medical robotics. I built a Unity-based hospital digital twin to 
simulate and compare sensor configurations across realistic clinical 
layouts before touching real hardware.

**Research Interests:** Real-time semantic segmentation, multi-sensor 
fusion, egocentric perception, deployment-aware deep learning, medical 
imaging AI, and robotic perception in safety-critical environments.

## Projects

- **EgoSeg-RT** [GitHub](https://github.com/Samarthraman01/EgoSeg-RT)  
  Real-time semantic segmentation on egocentric wearable camera footage. Benchmarked SegFormer across hardware and export formats. Quantified domain shift from ADE20K to VISOR-HOS egocentric kitchen footage.

- **SemanticBot** [GitHub](https://github.com/Samarthraman01/Semanticbot)  
  Open-vocabulary semantic 3D mapping pipeline using YOLO-World and CLIP, with a C++ ROS 2 fusion node producing a queryable semantic map in real time.

- **MediSeg** [GitHub](https://github.com/Samarthraman01/MediSeg)  
  Semantic segmentation built from scratch in PyTorch. Trained U-Net and SegFormer on ADE20K with full ONNX export pipeline.
